Attach the skill and assign an AI Index, and the agent looks up context first, then writes one query that works.

Why Elasticsearch? Every layer of this story is the same platform.

  • Your source data is already there.
  • The AI Index is just an Elasticsearch index.
  • Retrieving KIs is search: keyword, semantic, or both fused together with ES|QL FORK and FUSE.
  • ES|QL then queries the live data.
  • The agent's own traces land in Elasticsearch, which is how you measure the difference in Lab 4.

Retrieval ships as an out-of-the-box skill called ki-retrieval. In Lab 2 you attach it to a new agent.
