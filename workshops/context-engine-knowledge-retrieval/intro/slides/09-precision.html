You are on the customer-success team. Accounts are churning, and the answers are spread across three sources.

150articles

Knowledge base: title, category, content, summary.

S01Which Enterprise accounts have the highest churn risk, and what are they mostly calling us about?CRMTickets
S02Which Enterprise accounts renewing in the next 60 days have open P1 tickets, and which KB articles cover their issues?CRMTicketsKB
S03Which accounts have open P1 tickets and high churn risk right now?CRMTickets
S04What does the knowledge base say about our top support ticket categories?TicketsKB

The scenario: Precision Labs, a SaaS company with a churn problem. Three Elasticsearch indices: CRM accounts, support tickets, and knowledge base articles.

Four questions you will see all day. S02 is the one to watch: it crosses all three indices, so it is the hardest for an agent with no context.

Note the field names: the revenue field is called arr, deliberately terse, just like real data.
