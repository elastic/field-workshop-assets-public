Across the four questions, the Context Engine agent used about 60% fewer tokens and about 55% fewer LLM calls. The biggest win is on S02, the cross-index question: 21 calls down to 7.

S04 is a tie, and we say so. It is a simple knowledge-base question; the baseline agent already does fine with one search.

Be clear about the caveat: these are this workshop's data and agents. Real results depend on your data, your agent, and your usage. Elastic's own ElasticON material cites a similar figure, around 58%.
