Here is the hook: the numbers you will reproduce.

We ran five questions, five times each, on both agents. Across them, the Context Engine agent used about 56% fewer tokens and half the LLM calls, 60 down to 30 summed medians. The biggest cut is on S01: 18 calls down to 6.

Accuracy is the same: 20 of 25 answers correct for the baseline, 21 of 25 with Context Engine. We do not claim it is more accurate.

The real difference is predictability. On cross-index questions the baseline swings widely, 9 to 21 calls on S03. On S05, the single-index question, it stays between 5 and 9. In Lab 4 you will see every one of these runs.

Be clear about the caveat: these are this workshop's data and agents. Real results depend on your data, your agent, and your usage. Elastic's own ElasticON material cites a similar figure, around 58%.
