This is why Lab 4 exists. Same question, same two agents, five runs each, straight from the recorded eval.

The baseline took 9, 9, 10, 11 and 21 LLM calls. The Context Engine agent took 6, 6, 6, 8 and 12. If you compared one pair of runs, you could tell any story you like: the Context Engine saves 15 calls, or it costs 3.

So you repeat the runs and take the median: 10 versus 6. And you look at the spread.

Then you score every answer. Red squares are wrong answers. Fewer calls only matters if the answer is right, and on this question both agents sometimes miss accounts. We do not claim the Context Engine is more accurate.
