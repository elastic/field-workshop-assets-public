What it is

For each query: 1 ÷ the rank of the first right doc.

#1 = 1#2 = 0.5#5 = 0.2missed = 0

Then average over all your queries.

Why you care

One number for “how high does the right answer land?” 1.0 means always #1.

RAG and agents mostly read only the top few results, so #1 vs #5 is a right answer vs a wrong one.

MRR 0.425: on average the right doc lands around #2–#3, and one query missed it entirely.
