Answer (excerpt)

ILM moves indices through phases based on configurable criteria such as index age, size, or document count.

Hot: actively written to and searched; rollover creates a new index when thresholds are met (50GB, 30 days, 200 million docs). Warm: no longer written to but still queried; force merge and shrink apply. Cold: queried infrequently; searchable snapshots reduce local storage.
