Vector search basics · Indexing

Three ways to index vectors. Flat, HNSW, DiskBBQ.

01Flat
Exact. Cost grows with the data. Best for small or pre-filtered sets.
02HNSW
Approximate graph walk. Fast while the graph fits in RAM.
03DiskBBQ
k-means clusters; only the nearest clusters get scored. Vectors stay on disk.
